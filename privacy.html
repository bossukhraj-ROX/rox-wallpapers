Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how ROX handles information when you browse the website, contact support or purchase a digital wallpaper collection.

A clear overview

Information used with care.

ROX only uses information when it is needed to operate the website, respond to enquiries, support purchases and provide digital products. Payment processing is handled through Stripe Checkout.

  • Information You ProvideInformation you send through email or provide during a purchase may be used to respond to you and support your order.
  • Payment ProcessingPayments are processed by Stripe. ROX does not directly receive or store your full payment-card details.
  • Your ChoicesYou may contact ROX with privacy questions or requests relating to information you have provided directly.
The policy

Privacy information, organised clearly.

These sections explain the information ROX may receive, why it may be used and the choices available to you.

01

Information We May Receive

ROX may receive information you provide by email, contact details associated with support enquiries and purchase-related details needed to identify or support an order.

Basic technical or usage information may also be provided by hosting, security or website-service platforms where applicable.

02

How Information Is Used

  • Respond to enquiries and provide customer support
  • Help with purchase or download issues
  • Operate, improve and help secure the website
  • Meet legal or administrative obligations where required
03

Payments and Stripe

Purchases use Stripe Checkout. Stripe processes payment information, while ROX does not directly receive full payment-card details.

Stripe’s handling of information is governed by Stripe’s own privacy practices.

04

Cookies and Similar Technologies

The website itself may use essential browser storage or technologies needed for navigation, performance or security. Hosting or payment providers may use their own cookies or similar technologies when their services are used.

05

Sharing of Information

Information may be shared only where reasonably necessary with payment processors, website hosting providers and technical service providers, or with authorities where legally required.

ROX does not sell personal information.

06

Data Retention

Information is kept only for as long as is reasonably necessary for support, order administration, security, record keeping and legal obligations.

07

Data Security

ROX takes reasonable steps to protect information under its control. No internet service can guarantee complete security.

08

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights relating to access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection. You can contact ROX to make a request. Legal exceptions may apply.

09

Children’s Privacy

ROX is not intentionally directed at young children and does not knowingly seek personal information from children.

10

Third-Party Links and Services

The website may link to third-party services such as Stripe. Those providers control their own privacy practices, so their policies apply when you use their services.

11

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the website, services or legal requirements change. The policy date will be revised when changes are made.
