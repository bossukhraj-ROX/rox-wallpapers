Refund Policy

This policy explains how ROX handles refund requests for digital wallpaper purchases, including technical issues, duplicate purchases and completed downloads.

A clear overview

A fair approach to digital purchases.

ROX sells digital wallpaper collections. Because digital products can be accessed immediately after purchase, refunds are generally limited once access or delivery has been provided. ROX will still review genuine technical problems, duplicate purchases and other appropriate requests fairly.

  • Digital ProductsROX wallpaper collections are supplied digitally and may be available immediately after checkout.
  • Technical ProblemsIf a purchased collection cannot be accessed because of a genuine technical issue, contact ROX for support.
  • Duplicate PurchasesIf the same collection was purchased more than once by mistake, contact ROX with the relevant purchase details.
The policy

Refund information, organised clearly.

These sections explain the position for digital purchases and how ROX reviews genuine purchase-support requests.

01

Digital Product Purchases

ROX sells digital wallpaper collections, including Glass City. Digital products may be delivered or made accessible immediately after successful payment.

Once a digital product has been accessed, downloaded or supplied, a change-of-mind refund may not normally be available, subject to applicable consumer law.

02

Change-of-Mind Requests

Requests based only on preference, device appearance or a change of mind may not normally qualify after digital access has been provided. ROX encourages customers to review collection details and previews before purchase.

03

Technical Access Issues

Contact ROX if a collection cannot be accessed, a delivery method does not work, files appear incomplete or another genuine technical problem occurs.

ROX may first try to restore access, resend instructions or provide technical support before considering whether any further action is appropriate.

04

Duplicate Purchases

Accidental duplicate purchases may be reviewed. Please provide the email address used at checkout, relevant purchase details and a short explanation so ROX can identify the order.

Duplicate purchases are not approved automatically.

05

Incorrect or Incomplete Delivery

If you receive the wrong collection or an incomplete set, contact ROX with the relevant details. ROX may correct the delivery or provide the missing content before considering any refund.

06

Completed Downloads and Access

Refund eligibility may be limited after digital files have been received, accessed or downloaded. This does not remove any rights that may apply under consumer law.

07

Stripe and Payment Processing

Payment is processed through Stripe Checkout. Stripe handles payment processing, while ROX does not directly receive full card details.

Refund decisions relating to ROX products are reviewed by ROX. Where approved, a refund may be processed through the original payment method where possible.

08

Refund Requests

Send refund questions or requests to ROX by email. Include your name, the email used at checkout, the collection purchased, a clear explanation of the issue and any relevant purchase reference or receipt information.

roxhelpp@gmail.com
09

Review of Requests

Each request is reviewed based on the nature of the issue, whether digital access has already been supplied, whether technical support can resolve the problem, whether the purchase appears duplicated and applicable legal obligations.

10

Approved Refunds

Where a refund is approved, it will generally be returned using the original payment method where possible. Bank or payment-provider processing times can vary.

11

Chargebacks and Payment Disputes

Customers are encouraged to contact ROX first so an issue can be reviewed directly. This does not affect any payment-dispute rights that may apply to you.

12

Consumer Rights

Nothing in this policy is intended to limit any rights you may have under applicable consumer law.

13

Contact ROX

For refund questions or purchase-support requests, contact ROX by email and include the details needed to identify the purchase.

roxhelpp@gmail.com
