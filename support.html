Frequently asked questions

Helpful Answers.

Start here for clear guidance on digital purchases, downloads and contacting ROX.

How do I download my wallpapers?

After completing checkout, use the download page provided for your collection. Select the download button and save the file to your device before opening or extracting it.

What if my download doesn't work?

Contact ROX with the email address used at checkout, the collection purchased and a short description of what happened. This helps us review the issue and guide you clearly.

Can I download again later?

If you need help accessing a previously purchased collection, email ROX with your purchase details and we will review the best way to help.

I accidentally purchased twice.

Please contact ROX with the email used at checkout and any relevant purchase information. Duplicate-purchase requests are reviewed individually.

How do I contact ROX?

Email roxhelpp@gmail.com and include enough detail for us to identify the purchase or question you need help with.

Do upcoming collections become free?

Upcoming ROX collections are announced separately. Their availability and pricing will be shared when each collection is ready to release.
